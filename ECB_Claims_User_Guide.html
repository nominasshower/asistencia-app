EC Barton - ClaimsHow to report a problem with a vanity

This guide shows how to submit a claim when a vanity arrives with a problem. For each claim you send a photo of the vanity, a photo of its label, and you select the defects. It takes about two minutes from a phone, tablet or computer.

What you need

  • A phone, tablet or computer with Chrome or Safari and internet.
  • The access code above.
  • The order or PO number of the vanity.
  • Two photos: the vanity (showing the damage) and the label stuck on the vanity.
  • The number printed under the barcode on the label (you will type it; no scanning needed).

In short

  1. Open the link and enter the access code.
  2. Type the Order / PO number and your name.
  3. Add the photo of the vanity and the photo of the label, and type the barcode number of the label.
  4. Select the defect(s), add a comment if you wish, and tap Submit claim.
  5. Write down the claim number (for example ECB-00042) shown on the screen.
1

Open the page and enter the access code

Open the link on your phone, tablet or computer. Type the access code and tap Enter. The code is remembered on that device; use Sign out (top right) to remove it.
If it says Wrong access code, check the code and try again. If it still fails, contact Modular Tops.
2

Order and name; photo of the vanity

Type the Order / PO number and your name (both required).

Under Photo of the vanity, tap the button and take a photo, or choose one from your gallery. Show the whole vanity and the damaged area. You can tap the button again to retake it.
The photo is resized automatically, so it uploads fast even on a slow connection.
3

Photo of the vanity label and barcode number

Under Photo of the vanity label, take a photo of the label stuck on the vanity. The text and the barcode must be readable: get close, avoid glare and keep the phone steady.

Then type the number printed under the barcode in Barcode number on the label (required). Type it by hand; you do not need to scan it. Check it twice: this number is how we find the exact piece.
4

Select the defects and submit

Tick one or more defects (see the table below). Use Comments for anything we should know (optional).

When everything is complete, tap Submit claim. The button stays disabled, and a line under it lists what is still missing, until the order number, name, both photos, the barcode number and at least one defect are filled in.
Wait until the upload finishes. Do not close the page while it says Uploading photos and sending.
5

Keep your claim number

When the claim is received, the screen shows your claim number (for example ECB-00042). Write it down or take a screenshot: use it whenever you ask us about this claim.

To report another vanity, tap Submit another claim. Send one claim per vanity.

The defects

DefectWhat it means
Thin GelcoatThe glossy surface layer (gelcoat) is too thin or worn through in an area.
BubbleAir bubble in or under the surface.
CrackA crack or fissure in the vanity.
BrokenA piece is broken or missing.
PorositySmall pores or pinholes in the surface.
Clogged OverflowThe overflow opening of the sink is blocked.
Drain LeakWater leaks around the drain.

Tips and questions

  • Good photos: good light, no blur, no strong glare. The damage must be visible in the first photo and the label text readable in the second. Double-check the barcode number you type.
  • Upload failed or "Not sent": check your connection and tap Submit claim again. Your entries are still on the screen.
  • Camera or gallery: on a phone you can choose either one when you tap the photo button.
  • Photos are stored for 4 months and then deleted automatically. Your claim number and details stay on record.
  • Need help? Contact your Modular Tops representative.
